## GTA 6 trailer clips (shot 18)
Rockstar's trailers are their copyrighted material. Many channels use short clips for commentary and this is usually tolerated, but it is the most likely thing in the video to get a Content ID claim. If you use them, keep each clip short and talk over them instead of playing them silently. You can also leave the slides in place for that part.

## Avoid
- Clips from other YouTubers' videos. Those get claimed and can bring strikes.
- Leaked GTA 6 footage. Rockstar actively takes it down.
- Vice City's in-game radio music. Licensed songs get claimed straight away. Turn radio volume to 0 while recording.
